# The Secure Network — in plain words

This is the short version for anyone who just wants to know what the
button does. The detailed guide is in `docs/secure-network.md`.

## What is it?

A private road between your computer and Asian DVD Club. Regular internet
traffic takes the public highway; this gives club traffic its own
encrypted lane that only members can enter.

## What do I have to do?

One click. In Nebulae: **Settings → ADC Secure Network → Enroll**.
Your computer will show one permission prompt (on a Mac, one extra
"Allow" click). That is all. Nebulae does the rest by itself.

## Do I need to install or sign up for anything else?

No. No new accounts, no websites, no command line. Everything happens
inside Nebulae using your existing club membership.

## Is it safe?

Yes. Your computer keeps its own secret keys and never sends them to
anyone. The club's server can let you in or remove you — but can never
read your traffic. Downloads of club content get *more* private, not less.

## Will it slow down or change my other downloads?

No. Only traffic to the club uses the private road. Everything else —
other trackers, streaming, browsing — works exactly as before, at full
speed. If the club's network hiccups, your other downloads are not
touched.

## Can I say no?

Of course. The feature is optional. If you never click Enroll, nothing
changes at all. And if you try it and change your mind, one click
disconnects it.

## What's in it for me?

Faster, more reliable downloads of club content — especially if your
router is picky about incoming connections — plus the comfort of knowing
your member key travels encrypted.
